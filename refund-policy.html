Refund Policy

Cancel anytime. Refunds are easy.

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Cancel anytime

No contracts, no lock-in, and no cancellation fees. You can cancel your subscription whenever you like — just email us and we'll take care of it. Once you cancel, you won't be billed again after your current billing period ends.

Nothing to lose upfront

There's no setup fee, so you're never out a large deposit before you've even seen your site live. The only payment is the £39 monthly subscription — and even that comes with the no-hassle refund policy below.

Refunds, made simple

If you're not happy with your website or our service, email us at thedeveloperdudesllc@gmail.com and let us know. No forms, no "please explain in detail," no retention pitch — we'll refund your most recent payment back to your original payment method.

We built this policy because we'd rather earn your business every month than trap you in a contract. If it's not working for you, we don't want your money.

How long it takes

We aim to respond to every refund request within one working day. Refunds are typically processed within 5–10 working days, depending on your bank or card provider.

What happens after you cancel or get a refund

Once your subscription ends, hosting and maintenance for your site stop at the close of your current billing period. If you'd like a copy of your site's content before then, just let us know and we'll help you export it.

Questions?

Reach out any time at thedeveloperdudesllc@gmail.com or through our contact page — we're happy to help.
