About Us

Why we started Developer Dudes

Two engineers, tired of watching small businesses get overcharged and under-delivered by agencies.

We kept hearing the same story from small business owners: a website quote for thousands of dollars, a build that dragged on for months, and an agency that disappeared the moment the invoice was paid. Meanwhile, the actual work — a clean, fast, working website — usually takes a competent developer days, not months.

So we built Developer Dudes around a simple idea: real engineers, working directly with you, for one honest monthly price. No account managers relaying your feedback, no six-figure agency retainers, no disappearing after launch.

Jubair brings the software engineering — the code that makes a site fast and reliable. Muhtasim brings the security and infrastructure — the part that keeps it fast, secure, and actually online. Together, that's Developer Dudes.
