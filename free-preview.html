Frequently Asked Questions

Something else on your mind?

Is the website build really free?

Yes. There’s no design or build fee. We create your homepage preview for free, and if you decide to go ahead, we finish the website at no extra build cost. You pay £39/month for hosting, management and updates.

What if I don’t like my preview?

Tell us what you think and we can discuss the direction. You’re under no obligation to sign up, and you won’t be charged for the preview.

When does the £39/month payment start?

After you’ve seen your preview and decided to go ahead. You then join the hosting and management plan so we can finish the site and get it live. Requesting a preview doesn’t start a subscription.

What if I already have a website or domain?

That’s fine. Share your current website in the application. We can create a new design and connect it to your existing domain when it’s ready. If you need a domain, we’ll help you choose one and explain any registration costs before you commit.

Will my website show up on Google and AI search?

We set up on-site SEO, structure your service and location information, and submit your site for search engine indexing. This helps search engines and AI tools discover and understand it. Indexing, rankings and inclusion in AI answers are decided by those platforms, so specific placements aren’t guaranteed.

Can you make changes after the site goes live?

Yes. Ongoing website updates are included in the £39/month plan. Just message us with the changes you need, such as photos, services or contact details, and we’ll take care of them.

What do you need from me to get started?

Just your business name, trade and contact details. We’ll follow up on WhatsApp for anything else, such as your logo, photos and the services you’d like to feature.

How long will my preview take?

Once we’ve spoken about your business and have the details we need, we’ll confirm your preview timeline on WhatsApp. You’ll know what to expect before we start.
