Privacy Policy

What we collect, and why.

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Overview

This policy explains what information Developer Dudes collects when you use this website or contact us, and how we use it. We keep this simple on purpose — we only collect what we need to respond to you and provide our service.

Information we collect

When you fill out our contact form, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, if you provide one
  • Whatever message you send us

If you become a subscriber, we'll also need basic billing information to process your monthly payment. Payment details are handled by our payment processor — we don't store your card information ourselves.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and discuss your project
  • To build, host, and maintain your website if you subscribe
  • To send you billing-related communications
  • To improve how we communicate with prospective and current clients

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell or rent it to third parties.

Who we share it with

We only share your information with the service providers we rely on to run our business — for example, hosting and payment processing providers — and only as needed to deliver our service. We don't share your information with anyone else without your permission, unless required by law.

Cookies & tracking

This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. We may add basic, privacy-friendly analytics in the future to understand how visitors use the site; if we do, we'll update this policy.

Data retention

We keep contact form submissions and client information for as long as needed to respond to you or provide our service, and delete it when it's no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information we have about you at any time — just email us at thedeveloperdudesllc@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

Our service is intended for business owners and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email us at thedeveloperdudesllc@gmail.com.
